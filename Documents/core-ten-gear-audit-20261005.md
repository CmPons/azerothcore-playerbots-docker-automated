## Immediate opportunities before farming another raid

### 1. Ailina: an already-owned healing upgrade

She is wearing **Dragonspine Trophy** while Restoration. Its passive attack power and
melee/ranged-attack haste proc do not improve her normal healing role. She already owns
an **Eye of Gruul in her bags** (bag slot 23 in the captured bag), with spell power and a
healing-mana proc. That is the clearest role-correct swap in this audit. Keep Dragonspine
Trophy for a deliberate physical/off-spec setup; this is not a recommendation to sell it
or transfer a bound item to someone else.

Her other trinket, **Eye of Magtheridon**, supplies useful flat spell power, but its
spell-resist proc is aimed at offensive casting rather than healing. It is not statless;
it is simply not an ideal dedicated healer trinket. Her **idol slot is completely empty**,
including no spare idol found in the captured bags/bank armor/weapon inventory.

Her Malorne pieces are **Malorne Regalia**, the caster variant. Under Wrath's unified
spell-power itemization the stats can still suit healing; do not assume every
caster-labeled item is unusable. Equally, a high item level or a caster set bonus does
not automatically make it the best Restoration choice.

### 2. Tanks: defensive coverage and enchants, not just more purple pieces

**Redshift:** the snapshot reproduces **313 defense rating from equipment**, including
18 supplied by old equip spells, matching the supplied **482 Defense** character sheet.
No shield enchant is present. The installed **Enchant Shield - Defense (spell 44489)**
adds **20 defense rating**, enough to bring this unchanged set to 490 Defense. That is a
concrete non-drop route to the stated target, not an equipment change performed here.

Longer-term targets: replace **Medallion of Steadfast Might**, **Sickle of Unyielding
Strength**, and **Blessed Qiraji Musket** as suitable tank pieces arrive. Chain of Unleashed
Rage and Eternium Rage-shackles are offensive pieces, with no defense rating; tank-focused
alternatives offer another route to better survival. The Cobalt shield is a Wrath green
and should not be discarded solely for its color. **Syrannis' Mystic Sheen has 234 armor
and no attribute/equip-spell bonuses in this database**; that is its actual armor-heavy
itemization, not evidence of a missing random suffix or a broken loot conversion.

**Arinerica:** her equipment contributes approximately **233 defense rating**, including
Angelista's Touch's nine-rating equip effect. With saved base Defense 350, that implies
roughly **448 Defense before any uninspected bonuses**. She has no equipment resilience
in this snapshot. This is an equipment-derived warning, not a live buffed character-pane
measurement: her boss-tanking crit protection needs more attention than her two T5 pieces
might suggest. Her gems are largely stamina rather than defense.

Her Cobalt Helm alone contributes 50 defense rating; don't replace it merely to remove
a green. Keep the good Crystalforge tank pieces and Aldori Legacy Defender. Examine the
**Girdle of the Endless Pit**, **True-Aim Stalker Bands**, **Mark of Resolution**, and old
**Libram of Truth** when improving her tank setup. Mail hunter-style wrists trade defensive
stats/armor for offensive stats; there is no Cataclysm-style armor-specialization bonus
being assumed here.

### 3. Pilbok: weapon/talent alignment

The saved Combat build includes **Hack and Slash** and Improved Sinister Strike, while
his weapons are **Blade of the Unrequited + Emerald Ripper**, both daggers. Hack and Slash's
sword/axe benefit does not apply to them. A suitable slow sword/axe main hand matching
the current build is worth more attention than a small accessory-ilvl increase. Do not
blindly replace a good weapon with a dramatically lower-DPS one just for its type.

This audit establishes the mismatch, not why the bot chose or retained those weapons.
No talent rebuild, automatic equipment pass or AI policy change was performed.

### 4. Trinket roles across the casters/melee

- **Raney:** Eye of Gruul's healing proc is largely wasted on Frost DPS. Rejuvenating Gem
  still provides generic spell power and mana regeneration under Wrath, so its old healing
  label does not make it useless; better DPS trinkets would nevertheless help.
- **Beliona:** Eye of Gruul has the same proc-role problem. Talisman of Ascendance is old
  but has a genuinely relevant spell-power use effect; item level 60 alone is not a reason
  to throw it away without a replacement comparison.
- **Kaaren:** Tsunami Talisman is a good physical-DPS fit. Eye of Magtheridon can help the
  spell component of Enhancement, but a strong physical/hybrid DPS replacement is worth
  comparing. Her **Fang of Vashj and Malchazeen are both 1.8-speed daggers**; comparable
  slow Enhancement weapons would suit weapon strikes better. The high-ilvl Fang is not
  automatically worse than every slower, lower-tier weapon.

  has caster suffix stats; these are not empty-stat items. No full numeric comparison
  against the old staff in her bags was made.
- **Pilbok:** matching main hand, then low-level neck/wrists/cloak and trinkets. The three

**Bottom line:** the clears are entirely credible for this equipment. A handful of old
pieces are still useful, but there is more headroom in **role-correct trinkets, matching
weapons, defensive itemization and enchants** than the purple-item count alone suggests.
No need to replace the core ten or assume everyone needs a new full set.
